Phoebe Simulator

link connecting state - posture - battery - volume - playing nothing

Senses

Use headphones: with speakers the mic also hears the duck. The duck hears the mic only while "Mic on"; otherwise it hears silence.

Talk without the mic

Goes to lifed the way voiced sends what it hears: say the name to call it, then talk while it listens.

Touch and noises

ToF in the face

-

A hand close (under ~25 cm) is the theremin's; a person walking up shows as "near".

Power (the gamepad's Start)

The simulator presses Start at launch. Stopped (or with --no-policy), robotd holds its pose and lifed stays OFF.

Tilt the body

The fake IMU reports this gravity. Laid down for a moment, robotd calls it a fall.

Wi-Fi (pretend)

-

Show a Wi-Fi QR code for one of these to the camera after asking the duck to connect.
